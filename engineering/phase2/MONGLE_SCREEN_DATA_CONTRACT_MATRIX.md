# MONGLE_SCREEN_DATA_CONTRACT_MATRIX

> **AXIS: LEGACY_CURRENT_STATE (A).** Screen data needs are matched here
> against the *current* (largely legacy) API/DB. Where a row concludes
> `NO_CHANGE_REQUIRED`/`API_ADAPTER_REQUIRED` against a legacy endpoint
> (e.g. A1's `GET /api/players`, A3's `daily_points`), that conclusion is
> scoped to "does the legacy system already serve this UX need" and is
> **not** a statement that the underlying identity/table model is the
> target architecture. Do not treat any row here as evidence a legacy
> table/route should be `KEEP_AS_IS` in Axis B without checking
> `MONGLE_LEGACY_TO_TARGET_MAPPING.md` first.
